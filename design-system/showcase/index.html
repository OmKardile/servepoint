Principles

Six laws the app already obeys

Codified from the audit — each one is visible in production today.

1 · Honest first

Empty states explain the future. Deltas cite stored facts. Broken images vanish. Zero renders zero.

2 · Hairline does the work

Structure comes from 1px #E3E7E0 borders. Shadows whisper only on hover and overlay.

3 · Teal is the spine, gold is the ask

Deep teal structures every screen; brass gold appears only when we ask for action or mark the peak.

4 · Dense data, human voice

Poppins steps in 0.5px increments for instruments; Instrument Serif italic speaks the titles.

5 · Motion is a courtesy

120–160ms, press-scale 0.985, and every animation dies politely under prefers-reduced-motion.

6 · Touch is law

44px minimum interactive height — h-11 across the whole product, cashier gloves included.
